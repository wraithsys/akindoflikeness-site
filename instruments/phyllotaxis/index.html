There is no randomise button. STEP walks the roster forward and back along a fixed path, so anything you find can be returned to. The whole state reconstructs from its parameters, which is why the address bar is a preset — copy the URL and it carries the patch.

Each entry brings its own scale. The degrees are computed from that algorithm's own spectrum by minimising sensory dissonance, so the more inharmonic a voice is, the further its scale sits from 12-TET — and the more consonant it sounds. Two entries have so few partials that they prefer a handful of intervals and are sounded whole; those are marked chord rather than scale.
