PHYLLOTAXIS

Specimen sheet 01
What the platform is for

This is not an audit of whether the house rules survive a change of technology. The 1-bit laws were never taste — they were what egui's grain made worth doing: immediate mode with no previous frame to read, a CPU raster, and a widget system that charges for every pixel of state. Design with that machine and you arrive at dither, hard rectangles and no antialiasing, because those are the things it is good at.

WebGL's grain is the opposite on every one of those axes. So the honest move is not to re-adopt the old constraints — it is to find out what this machine is good at by running it past where it is comfortable, and let the rules fall out of that. Several specimens below deliberately break house law. That is the point of them. Drag anything that moves.

egui gives you
A rectangle and a CPU. Every pixel costs, so scarcity is the material — and scarcity is what dither, hard edges and 1-bit are answers to.
WebGL gives you
A per-pixel program running a hundred thousand times in parallel, a previous frame you can read, and float precision. Abundance is the material.
So the question changes
Not "how little can this be drawn with" but "what is worth computing per pixel, per frame, that a value could not have carried."
01

Four tone regimes, one field — the constraint made choosable

The same golden-angle interference field, rendered four ways across one canvas. Left to right: 1-bit ordered dither, the house answer. Four-level. Full continuous tone, which egui can do but never cheaply. And accumulated bloom, which it cannot do at all.

Nothing here argues for one. It makes the choice a choice — the 1-bit band should have to win against the others on this screen rather than by inheritance.

What to look for Whether the dithered band still reads as the most alive of the four. On a CPU raster it was the one that could move; here all four can. If it wins anyway, it has earned the law back on this platform.
02

Feedback as a medium, not an effect

The previous frame is a texture. Fade it, warp the coordinates you sample it at, and draw the new beam on top — and the buffer stops being a trail and becomes a fluid the instrument is drawing into. Rotate, scale and advect at once and it never resolves to a shape, because every frame is the last one seen through a small distortion.

This is the single largest capability gap. Immediate mode has no previous frame by definition, so nothing in this family has ever been able to accumulate. Push warp past the middle and it stops being a scope and starts being weather.

φ 1.62
Breaks house law deliberately Continuous tone, additive blending, no hard edge anywhere. Ask whether the 1-bit resolve (the checkbox) improves it or just makes it look like the other instruments. That answer is a design decision, not a compliance check.
03

Population — where the count stops being a number

Seeds placed at the golden angle, positions computed on the GPU from nothing but an index. Take the count up. Somewhere past twenty thousand the individual seed stops being visible and the parastichies — the counter-rotating spiral arms, which are always consecutive Fibonacci numbers — take over as the thing you see.

That transition is the instrument's whole subject rendering itself, and it is only reachable at a scale a CPU raster cannot draw. The count slider goes to 250,000. Nothing is stored; every position is n put through two trig calls.

40,000 · φ exact
What it earns Detune the angle a fraction of a degree off φ and the arms reorganise into a different Fibonacci pair, then collapse. That is a visualisation of irrationality — and it is the same quantity the tuning engine uses to decide how inharmonic a voice is. One control, two senses.
04

Colour as a continuous quantity

House law rations colour to state. That law was written where colour was expensive and a palette was a handful of chosen values. Here hue is a float, computed per pixel, and it can carry a continuous quantity that no number on a panel could show at once.

This band maps the dissonance curve — the real one, the Plomp–Levelt sum the tuning crate minimises — across an octave. Valleys are where the spectrum wants to be played. The scale degrees are read straight off the minima, and they are drawn where they fall.

harmonic · 7 degrees
Breaks house law deliberately Colour here is not state, it is magnitude — and a magnitude that changes shape as you drag. Slide inharmonicity from 0 to 100 and watch the valleys migrate off the 12-TET grid. Toggle "rationed" to see the same information in the house's monochrome and judge what was lost.
05

The control — discrete value, continuous travel

The thing you liked about the effort selector, isolated. The value snaps to real stops; the drawing never does. Drag it. The readout shows what the instrument holds and, separately, where the thumb has got to — so the disagreement is visible rather than felt.

Turn on show the lie: the hairline marks the true value during flight. For a quantised parameter the gap is honest — the sound is at one stop or the other and the travel is the eye catching up. Switch to a continuous parameter and the same gap becomes a lie about the sound. That distinction is the only thing here that has to become a rule.

VALUE 4/8 · THUMB 4.00
Proposed, at LEANING Travel may lag a value only where the value is quantised. Failure it prevents: an eight-position algorithm selector that jumps reads as a mode switch, and the player stops hearing the eight as one axis. Confidence: high on the shape, low on the chase constant — that one wants your hand on it, not an argument.

What this sheet is actually for

  1. Find the ceiling before picking a style. 03 goes to a quarter of a million points because the interesting thing was not visible until it did. None of the numbers here are settings — they are the range the platform turned out to have.
  2. Let 02 and 04 stay illegal for a while. Feedback and continuous colour are the two things this machine does that the others cannot. Deciding against them early would be inheritance wearing the clothes of a decision.
  3. Only 05 is proposing a rule, and only at LEANING, because it is the only one with a named failure behind it.
