Harmony Tastings · The Synthesis · 2026-08-21

THE RUB AND
THE RETURN

Billy's harmonic taste, extracted from ~150 of his own verdicts: eight traversals, three decks, every law below backed by something he played and judged. Nothing acts on this yet — it is the picture, as agreed.

Each law carries its confidence: iron means confirmed repeatedly and never contradicted; firm means well-supported; edge marks the actual boundary of the taste, where verdicts wobble — which is knowledge too.

LAW 1 · ironThe ear configures around the bass.

Billy's word for it: the context note. Whatever sounds, the lowest note decides what it means. He proved it on himself:

"Sounds min so i think yes my ear configure around Bass" — on hearing Fmaj7/E read as an E-rooted minor thing

Corroborated twice without prompting: D♯maj9 over a D bass → "minor"; a C13 shell over D → "I hear min7." Consequence: every other law reads from the bass, and notation for Billy should too.

LAW 2 · ironOne drop of vinegar, never the bottle.

Nearly every chord he keeps carries exactly one semitone rub inside an otherwise warm frame. The most loved chord in the corpus is the tightest possible version — C4 C♯4 F4 — a major-seventh colour delivered as touch, not as a jazz stack:

"I LOVE THIS"— first tasting session

But the pure cluster got a joke ("penguin stack :P"), and rubs that slide to new rubs without arriving got binned. The rub is a point of friction inside something that holds it — not dissonance for its own sake.

LAW 3 · ironChords are states with exits. The taste lives on moves.

The blind chord deck — 24 probes including planted horrors — came back 41 keeps, zero bins, across two full passes. His traversal name explained why:

"there is nothing i've heard that doesn't resolve with one added or changed note"— after the static deck

No vertical is rejected as a vertical, because none is heard as an object — each is a place with somewhere to go. Judgment begins only when something moves. The first bins in the entire project arrived the moment the deck started dealing transitions.

LAW 4 · ironConnective tissue is the one unforgivable thing.

His phrase, coined while binning: "no connective tissue." A move whose voices share nothing — no common tone, no semitone logic — is dead, and nothing redeems it: not continuing (binned), not returning home afterwards (binned twice, "ew no point to that"), not landing somewhere beautiful. Law 3's mercy applies to chords, never to broken transitions. And connection is spectral, not just harmonic — the same beloved sonority jumped an octave was binned:

"they felt disconnected from one another — not in pitch but tone" — on a register leap between two loved chords
LAW 5 · firmA move must carry a promise.

Every binned move shares one property: no implied next. Every kept move points somewhere — including moves that raise tension, because the promise is the pleasure:

"exited for chord 3"— keeping a move whose arrival was a new tension

He judges move n by whether move n+1 exists. Which is also why the only place the judge-it-closed discipline broke was a maximally open ending: "ALL I CAN HEAR IS CHORD FOUR … all options open because the space is undefined."

LAW 6 · ironMajor at rest is the one forbidden colour.

The single most confirmed result: a plain major arrival was binned four times and never once kept — "dont like it", "no", "nah". Yet emptiness is a valid home: bare fifths and octaves as arrivals got "Yah", "yum", "nice". So it isn't consonance he refuses — it's the major third's particular sweetness at the moment of rest. When asked what should arrive instead of D major, he composed the answer:

"my ear hears D3 A3 D4 G♯4 A4 C5 for chord 3" — the ♯11 and ♭7 glowing over the root: his arrival colour
LAW 7 · firmHome is a bass condition, not a chord type.

Three different homes appear in the corpus — D♯madd9 ("That's home"), root-position Fmaj7 ("Here"), plain Dm ("home") — different qualities, one constant: root position, bass equal to root. The first plain triad in ~150 verdicts appears at the word "home", arrived at by an inner voice walking B→C→C♯→D ("final stretch → almost → so close → home"). Consonance is function-gated: a plain chord is a cadence, never a dwelling. And a shaped tension can be an ending too — the open-ended journey landing on his own Esus7 was kept: "differnt but nice." One composition law came free:

"i usually like to keep the bass out until home lands" — the bass entry is the arrival marker
LAW 8 · firmParallel motion is a gesture, not a technique.

The whole home chord planed up a semitone: "house of horrors — suspense" — kept. Planed three steps, Dm→D♯m→Em: "nice" — kept. There and back, Dm→D♯m→Dm: "Delightful — a return is fine, it matters more what the accent is." But the same material planed down an octave: "pointless" (with the honest caveat: "works with reese type sounds — in the environment no"). Semitone planing tells a story; octave planing merely repeats one.

LAW 9 · edgeThe boundary, as measured.

Where verdicts wobbled, the fence runs: a chromatic bass climb to a minor arrival that isn't home was first "a juicy movement nonetheless" (keep), later "not really my thing" (bin). Endings that are neither home nor shaped-tension sit exactly on the line. Also on method: when button and word disagreed (one journey clicked KEEP but worded "pointless"), the word wins — his vocabulary is the instrument, the button is just a button.

THE LEXICON
  • context note — the bass; the note the ear configures around. All degrees read from it.
  • sus7 — his coinage: root + fifth + ♭7, the third withheld. The books write 7(no3); the ledger writes his word. He now uses it in slash notation unprompted ("Dsus7/A").
  • connective tissue — what a move must have; its absence is the only unforgivable fault.
  • the rub — one semitone adjacency glowing inside a warm frame; his signature interval, vertically in chords and horizontally as the approach to home.
  • traversal — an exploration saved whole, in the order it was walked; his words on them are form-words: basis, step, return, interruption, detour, question, answer, home.
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR PHYLLOTAXIS

Nothing has been changed — this is for the conversation, per the standing rule. But the convergences are worth naming: the cadence engine's bias tables already avoid thirds ("triads are coincidences") — his hands independently confirmed the code's taste. Where the engine and the ear disagree: the director's voicing vocabulary includes plain triads (Law 6 says never at rest), roots move continuously (Law 7 suggests the bass be withheld and spent on arrivals), and nothing in the engine currently guarantees connective tissue across a voicing change (Law 4 says that is the one fault that cannot be redeemed). When it's time to go from here, those three are the doors.
