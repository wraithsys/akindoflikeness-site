BYPO

blow your phase off — a monophonic fm drone synth. rust, MIT, no envelopes. the source is public and the Windows build is free.

open ▸ download · windows v1.0.2

PHYLLOTAXIS

a polyphonic instrument that plays itself, in the browser. nothing to download. its scales are computed from each algorithm’s own spectrum rather than assumed — so the more inharmonic a voice gets, the more consonant it sounds. when the stream is on it plays itself on youtube, and the chat plays it too: shape a patch, press share, paste the link into the chat — the stream takes it, with your name on it.

play in browser ▸ stream · youtube no install

also in the works fibonacci-drum · the dome · constellation
