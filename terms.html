A purchase is a perpetual licence — one payment, no subscription, no expiry — for personal use, active on a limited number of machines at once (the activation cap stated on the product page). It is not transferable to another person.

THE MAILING LIST

The "keep me in the loop" form stores three things: your email address, the time you signed up, and a private token used only to build your unsubscribe link. That's the whole record. It is not shared with anyone, and nothing about a purchase is ever added to it — the mailing list and Lemon Squeezy's order records are two separate systems that never meet.

  • Unsubscribe — the link in any mail we send removes you immediately, no login, no confirmation screen.
